Alestur Wolf

Species: Wood Elf
Age: 28 yrs
Class: Gloom Stalker Ranger
Story: Abandoned by unknown parents and shaped by the unforgiving streets of Vladrich, Alestur became a cunning master of its alleys and shadows. Life taught him one lesson: he stood alone. That changed when he met Alexandria, a high elf he found begging in Elara’s Capitol. She was the first to show him there was more to life than survival.

Alexandria Willthorne

Species: High Elf
Age: 32 yrs
Class: Way of Mercy Monk
Story: After her parents were killed in a feud she never knew existed, Alexandria wandered for years, struggling to survive. In Elara’s Capitol, exhausted and alone, she was harassed and beaten by bored patrolmen. Alestur, carrying more trauma than patience, intervened. Their meeting marked the start of a bond neither expected.

Grogth

Species: Half-Orc
Age: 42 yrs
Class: Path of the Ancestral Guardian Barbarian
Story: Born to an orc mother and a human father, Grogth grew up an outcast among a clan famed for honoring their ancestors through conquest. For years he wandered Elara and Wustoria, seeking his own way to honor them. At an Elarian outpost, he saw residents slaughtered by guards and rescued two trapped children. An arrow suddenly struck a guard attacking him—Alestur had arrived, with Alexandria close behind. They fought to protect the innocent, giving help while asking nothing. Inspired, Grogth realized that serving the meek was also serving his ancestors. Though Alestur initially distrusted him—hating humans and orcs who preyed on the weak—Grogth’s sincerity earned him a place among them, eventually becoming the brother Alestur never expected.

Wilks Adams

Species: Human
Age: 16 yrs
Class: Undiscovered
Story: Power always crushes the powerless—nothing new. All I cared about was protecting my sister, Seriah, though I was rarely strong enough to do it. No one ever helped us, so I stopped believing anyone could. Then came the day everything changed. Three strangers—an elf couple and an orc—threw themselves into danger to save us. For the first time in my life, I owed someone something, and I’m grateful it was them.

Seriah Adams

Species: Human
Age: 13 yrs
Class: Undiscovered
Story: My brother always held my hand tightly, scanning for the “monsters” he feared would find us. To him, it was always the two of us against the world. When a group of men cornered us, he pulled me behind him, whispering for me to stay quiet. I shut my eyes, terrified—until a change in the air made me look. An elf woman with leaf-like ears stood beside a sharp-eyed elf and a towering orc. A single growl from the orc sent the men fleeing. The elf woman’s gentle smile eased my fear, but it was my brother’s stunned disbelief that I remember most. He had always told me no one would ever help us. But these strangers did. From then on, we became a strange kind of family, and though we called one another siblings, it was clear that Alestur and Alexandria had become the parents we never had.
