The Family

The strongest bonds have nothing to do with blood.

Scroll down to meet them, one by one.

Member 01 · The Ranger

Alestur Wolf

Wood Elf 28 yrs Gloom Stalker Ranger

Abandoned by unknown parents and shaped by the unforgiving streets of Vladrich, Alestur became a cunning master of its alleys and shadows. Life taught him one lesson: he stood alone. That changed when he met Alexandria, a high elf he found begging in Elara’s Capitol. She was the first to show him there was more to life than survival.

Member 02 · The Monk

Alexandria Willthorne

High Elf 32 yrs Way of Mercy Monk

After her parents were killed in a feud she never knew existed, Alexandria wandered for years, struggling to survive. In Elara’s Capitol, exhausted and alone, she was harassed and beaten by bored patrolmen. Alestur, carrying more trauma than patience, intervened. Their meeting marked the start of a bond neither expected.

Member 03 · The Guardian

Grogth

Half-Orc 42 yrs Ancestral Guardian Barbarian

Born to an orc mother and a human father, Grogth grew up an outcast among a clan famed for honoring their ancestors through conquest. For years he wandered Elara and Wustoria, seeking his own way to honor them. At an Elarian outpost, he saw residents slaughtered by guards and rescued two trapped children. An arrow suddenly struck a guard attacking him — Alestur had arrived, with Alexandria close behind. Inspired, Grogth realized that serving the meek was also serving his ancestors, and eventually became the brother Alestur never expected.

Member 04 · The Brother

Wilks Adams

Human 16 yrs Class Undiscovered

Power always crushes the powerless — nothing new. All I cared about was protecting my sister, Seriah, though I was rarely strong enough to do it. No one ever helped us, so I stopped believing anyone could. Then came the day everything changed. Three strangers — an elf couple and an orc — threw themselves into danger to save us. For the first time in my life, I owed someone something, and I’m grateful it was them.

Member 05 · The Sister

Seriah Adams

Human 13 yrs Class Undiscovered

My brother always held my hand tightly, scanning for the “monsters” he feared would find us. When a group of men cornered us, he pulled me behind him, whispering for me to stay quiet. I shut my eyes — until a change in the air made me look. An elf woman with leaf-like ears stood beside a sharp-eyed elf and a towering orc. A single growl from the orc sent the men fleeing. From then on, we became a strange kind of family — and though we called one another siblings, Alestur and Alexandria had become the parents we never had.
